Occupational head protection		 Active 2026-02-12

View details

Scope: Specifies minimum performance, construction, inspection, and marking requirements for industrial safety helmets intended to protect workers from falling objects and incidental impact in routine industrial and construction environments.

Applicability: Applies to industrial safety helmets supplied for general workplace use; excludes helmets specifically designed for high-voltage electrical insulation, firefighting, sports, and motorcycling.

Certification: Certification applicable where the referenced product control order is in force; otherwise this standard may be used for voluntary product conformity claims.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-20

Amendment status: Amended | Reference: AMD-DEMO-1001-01

Amendment: Amendment 1 (AMD-DEMO-1001-01)

Date: 2026-07-15 | Effective: 2026-09-01

Affected clause: Clause 7 - Marking and traceability

Previous: Required the manufacturer identification and product model marking on the shell or label.

Revised: Adds a batch or lot traceability mark and allows the mark to be placed on the shell or durable internal label.

Reason: Improves traceability for production batches and field investigations without changing the core safety performance criteria.

Key requirements:

  • Shell material shall maintain structural integrity after impact testing.
  • Helmet suspension shall retain the shell and maintain clearance from the headform.
  • Chin-strap attachment, where provided, shall meet the specified retention performance.
  • Manufacturer shall control dimensions, materials, and production consistency.

Testing requirements:

  • Impact attenuation test using specified impact energies and headform configuration.
  • Penetration resistance test using a controlled striker.
  • Retention-system performance test where a retention system is supplied.
  • Environmental conditioning before selected performance tests.

Laboratory/test evidence:

  • Product test report identifying sample configuration and conditioning.
  • Test observations and measured impact/penetration results.
  • Laboratory statement linking sample identification to the tested production model.

Marking requirements:

  • Manufacturer or responsible brand identification.
  • Model or product identification.
  • Standard number: IS DEMO 1001:2026.
  • Manufacturing batch or traceability mark.

Documentation required:

  • Product specification sheet.
  • Bill of materials or material declaration.
  • Routine production inspection record.
  • Relevant laboratory test report.

Supersession: No direct supersession relationship

Electric Storage Water Heaters - Domestic Safety and Energy Performance		 Electrical Appliances
Domestic water heating		 Active 2025-09-14
Effective: 2025-12-01		 QCO-DEMO-ELEC-2025-05
View details

Scope: Specifies electrical safety, thermal performance, pressure containment, controls, energy-performance declaration, and marking requirements for domestic electric storage water heaters.

Applicability: Applies to fixed electric storage water heaters for domestic hot-water service within the declared capacity range; excludes instantaneous heaters and industrial hot-water vessels.

Certification: Certification applicable where a relevant product control order adopts this standard; otherwise conformity assessment may be voluntary or procurement-driven.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-12

Amendment status: No amendment | Reference: None

Amendment: No amendment.

Key requirements:

  • Tank and pressure-containing parts shall withstand the specified pressure condition.
  • Electrical protection shall address insulation, leakage, and protective-device operation.
  • Thermostatic controls shall maintain the declared water-temperature range.
  • Energy-performance information shall be supported by the stated test method.

Testing requirements:

  • Pressure containment and leakage test.
  • Dielectric strength and insulation tests.
  • Thermostat and safety cut-out function test.
  • Energy-performance or heat-loss verification.

Laboratory/test evidence:

  • Pressure and leakage test report.
  • Electrical safety report.
  • Thermostat/safety cut-out functional test record.
  • Energy-performance test results.

Marking requirements:

  • Manufacturer identification.
  • Rated capacity and electrical data.
  • Model and serial/batch identification.
  • Installation warnings and pressure-related information.

Documentation required:

  • Tank construction specification.
  • Electrical schematic and component list.
  • Installation and operating manual.
  • Laboratory safety and energy-performance evidence.

Supersession: No direct supersession relationship
